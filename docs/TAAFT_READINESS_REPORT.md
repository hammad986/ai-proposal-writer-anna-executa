## 4. Product Review Brief for Anna Co-Founder Dora

**Subject:** AI Proposal Writer 2.0 — Major Architecture & Feature Upgrade (RFP Intelligence, Word .docx, & Compliance Matrix)

Hi Dora,

Following your valuable advice regarding competitor benchmarking and product completeness, we have completed a comprehensive engineering overhaul of **AI Proposal Writer (now v2.0.0)**.

Rather than cosmetic changes, we conducted in-depth competitor research across PandaDoc, Qwilr, Responsive (RFPIO), and Loopio to engineer genuine enterprise differentiators:

1. **AI RFP Shredder & Requirements Intelligence**: Users can now upload or paste client RFPs, statements of work, or emails. The system shreds the document to extract mandatory compliance clauses (`shall`, `must`), technical constraints, milestones, and commercial terms.
2. **2-Way RFP Compliance Matrix**: Generates a live compliance matrix linking client requirements directly to proposal sections, highlighting coverage gaps, and allowing 1-click affirmative clause injection to ensure proposals never get disqualified.
3. **Company Knowledge Base (Local RAG)**: Persists verified agency case studies, security certifications (SOC2, HIPAA), and team architect profiles in Anna Storage, grounding generation in real facts rather than generic hallucinations.
4. **Native Microsoft Word (.docx) + Vector PDF Exports**: Added complete multi-format export parity. RFP teams can now download clean, editable `.docx` files formatted with metadata tables, styled headings, and signature stamps alongside our vector A4 PDF.
5. **Interactive Buyer CPQ & Financial Modeling**: Dynamic package selection, multi-currency FX converter (9 currencies), and quantitative ROI calculation.

All 33 automated tests are passing, and the application compiles cleanly under `@anna-ai/app-schema` dispatcher 0.22.0.

We would love to share this updated build with you for re-review and discuss next steps for platform distribution and the Founding Builder Program.
